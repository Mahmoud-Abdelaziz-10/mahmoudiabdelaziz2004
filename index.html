Background & Approach

Recent Graduate Engineer

As a dedicated graduate in Landscape and Irrigation Engineering, I bridge the gap between creative outdoor spatial aesthetics and rigorous hydraulic infrastructure. My academic background and extensive practice projects have focused on developing buildable, sustainable, and water-efficient landscape designs.

Every drawing and 3D visualization featured in this portfolio reflects hands-on training across residential villa concepts, 2D AutoCAD construction details, irrigation piping and zoning calculations, and 3D modeled garden walk-throughs.

I am seeking entry-level engineering and drafting opportunities where I can apply my CAD proficiency, learn on active sites, and contribute to forward-thinking landscape developments.
